MILL LIMITS 
Hot-rolled steels are available in thicknesses of 0.071 -0.75 
in. (1.80-19.05 mm) and widths of 30-80 in. (1016-2032 mm) 
depending on dimension and product quality. For sizes 
outside of these limits, please contact your Cleveland-Cliffs 
sales representative.
The standard inner diameter of hot-rolled pickled coils is 24 
in. (610 mm), while hot bands have inside diameters of 30 in. 
(762 mm). 
Thickness, width, and flatness tolerances are covered in 
ASTM A568 or A635.
