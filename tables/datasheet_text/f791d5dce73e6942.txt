C11000 (Cu-ETP)

18 08  US
Aurubis designations: C110 ● PNA211 
UNS C11000 ● EN CW004A ● JIS C110 
Electrolytic Tough Pitch Copper (ETP) is the most widely used of the coppers because of 
its combination of electrical and thermal conductivity, corrosion resistance, workability and 
aesthetic beauty. The superb corrosion resistance makes it a favored material for building 
applications and when exposed to weather for long periods, even centuries, this copper will 
develop a relatively impervious protective film which eventually becomes the familiar green 
patina of weathered copper. The beauty and ease of finishing make this copper a favorite 
for articles in the home.

Description 

Electrolytic Tough Pitch Copper (ETP) is the most widely used of the coppers because of 
its combination of electrical and thermal conductivity, corrosion resistance, workability and 
aesthetic beauty. The superb corrosion resistance makes it a favored material for building 
applications and when exposed to weather for long periods, even centuries, this copper will 
develop a relatively impervious protective film which eventually becomes the familiar green 
patina of weathered copper. The beauty and ease of finishing make this copper a favorite 
for articles in the home.
