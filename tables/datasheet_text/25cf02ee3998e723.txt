The tolerances in the tables below apply to parts produced by metal removal or formed from sheet metal. ISO 2768-1 only applies to those linear dimensions (for example external sizes, internal sizes, step sizes, diameters, radii, distances, external radii and chamfer heights for broken edges) which do not have an individual tolerance indicated on the drawing. When general tolerances in accordance with ISO 2768-1 shall apply, the drawing should be marked in or near the title block with the notation ISO 2768 followed by the tolerance class, for example ISO 2768-m .
