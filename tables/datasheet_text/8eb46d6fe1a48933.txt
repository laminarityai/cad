alloys. Corrosion resistance and appearance after a nodizing are superior to all other screw machine alloys while strength  
is the lowest.

faying surfaces.

4 Ratings A through D for Weldability and Brazeabil ity are relative ratings defined as follows: 
 A= Generally weldable by all commercial procedures  and methods. 
 B= Weldable with special techniques or for specifi c applications that justify preliminary trials or testing to develop welding procedure and weld performance.
