## Clearance versus tapped holes
<!-- keys: clearance, through hole, screw, bolt, tapped, close medium coarse -->
A clearance hole allows the screw to pass through without engaging an internal thread. A tapped hole is prepared for an internal thread. Choose the intended role before looking up a diameter.

The cited source is a secondary summary of DIN EN 20273 / ISO 273. Its fine, medium and coarse clearance series describe hole sizes, not screw thread pitches.

## Choosing a clearance series
<!-- keys: fine clearance, close fit, medium fit, coarse fit, tolerance -->
The source labels its series fein, mittel and grob; the catalog maps them to close, medium and coarse. Preserve the selected series when reading the exact fastener key. A clearance series is distinct from a dimensional tolerance class.

The source table contains more sizes than the product table. A source entry does not itself add a supported fastener key.

## What the CAD holes check establishes
<!-- keys: holes check, limitations, thread strength, engagement -->
Product integration note: the holes check measures round-hole diameters and count. The requirement selects the exact fastener-table key and intended fit. Diameter evidence alone does not establish thread engagement, preload, stripping strength or a manufactured thread profile. Non-round openings may leave the result unknown.
