## Select a compound for the application
<!-- keys: rubber compound, fluid compatibility, seal hardness -->
Parker distinguishes elastomer families from formulated compounds. Fluid, temperature and mechanical demands govern selection; hardness alone does not identify a compound's suitability.

Catalog application: record the exact supplier compound, medium, concentration, temperature range and static or moving service before accepting compatibility evidence. See [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md).

## NBR nitrile and Buna-N
<!-- page: 69 -->
<!-- keys: NBR, nitrile rubber, Buna N, mineral oil seals -->
Parker describes NBR as an acrylonitrile-butadiene rubber used with oils and fuels. Acrylonitrile content affects behavior; ozone and weather resistance are limitations. Confirm the particular compound and fluid rather than transferring a family rating.

## EPDM water and weather service
<!-- page: 71 -->
<!-- keys: EPDM, ethylene propylene rubber, steam seals, glycol brake fluid -->
Parker identifies EPDM candidates for hot water, steam, glycol brake fluids and weather exposure, but excludes mineral oils, greases and fuels. Temperature capability remains compound-specific.

## FKM fluorocarbon selection
<!-- page: 71-72 -->
<!-- keys: FKM, fluorocarbon rubber, fuel seals, fluoroelastomer -->
Parker describes FKM resistance to heat, oils, fuels and ozone. Verify chemical compatibility and low-temperature flexibility for the actual compound; the family name is not a universal fluid approval.

## Silicone VMQ selection
<!-- page: 73-74 -->
<!-- keys: VMQ, silicone rubber, LSR, tear resistance -->
Parker describes silicone's useful temperature flexibility but limited tear and wear performance. VMQ silicone and FVMQ fluorosilicone are distinct families; do not transfer compatibility between them.

## Seal geometry and evidence limits
<!-- keys: gland, compression set, squeeze, extrusion gap, dynamic seal -->
Parker treats material choice alongside gland design, deformation and extrusion control. A compatible elastomer alone does not establish a working seal.

Catalog policy: this guide supplies selection context, not groove dimensions, squeeze limits, pressure ratings or a sealing check. Use the relevant handbook application and supplier compound data for the actual joint. Missing values remain unknown. See [fits and tolerances](fits-and-tolerances.md) and [sliding contact](sliding-contact-and-wear.md).
