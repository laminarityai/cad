## PA6 and PA66 are distinct stock materials
<!-- keys: polyamide, unfilled, extruded rod, plate, cast nylon -->
Ensinger identifies TECAMID 6 natural as unfilled extruded PA6 and TECAMID 66 natural as unfilled extruded PA66. These are distinct polymer grades, with different mechanical and moisture responses. Keep the polymer designation and manufacturing route; this guide does not establish equivalence to cast nylon or a filled grade.

## Selection for machined and sliding components
<!-- keys: gears, rollers, pulleys, sliding pads, wear, machining -->
Ensinger lists gears, rollers, pulleys and sliding pads among extruded-nylon applications, describing machinability and useful sliding and wear behavior. Treat these as candidate uses. The exact material pair and operating conditions still need evaluation for a loaded moving part.

For manufacturing context, see [CNC guidance](cnc-design-guidance.md).

## PA6 and PA66 moisture conditioning and fit
<!-- keys: dry nylon, conditioned nylon, humidity, water absorption, swelling -->
Ensinger distinguishes the moisture response of different polyamides. Record whether property specimens and finished parts are dry or conditioned; do not assume that an accurately machined dry part retains the same fit in service.

Supplemental source: [Mitsubishi Chemical dimensional-stability guidance](https://www.mcam.com/en/support/material-properties/dimensional-stability), moisture exposure and dimensional change. See [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md).
