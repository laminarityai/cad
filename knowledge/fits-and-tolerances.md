## Clearance transition and interference fits
<!-- keys: clearance fit, transition fit, interference fit, mating limits -->
A fit describes the relationship between mating size limits. Clearance permits space between the mating features, interference requires overlap of their free sizes, and a transition fit can produce either depending on actual sizes. Evaluate the paired limits, not just the nominal dimensions.

Source scope: [ISO 286-1 public description](https://www.iso.org/standard/45975.html), tolerances, deviations and fits. This is an original conceptual explanation, not a reproduced standard definition or fit table.

## A hole tolerance is only part of the specification
<!-- keys: H7 hole, shaft tolerance, tolerance zone, hole basis -->
A hole class supplies only the hole side of a mating-size specification. Establish the mating shaft limits and application needs before selecting a fit. The product's [hole-class guide](iso-hole-tolerance-classes.md) identifies the available hole keys; it does not provide a complete shaft-fit catalog.

Source scope: ISO 286-1 addresses size tolerances for cylinders and opposing parallel surfaces. No standard's numerical limits are reproduced here.

## Geometric controls and inspection intent
<!-- keys: GD&T, datum, position tolerance, orientation, form, inspection -->
Size limits alone do not fully communicate feature location or orientation. ASME's public overview identifies datum references, position, profile, orientation and form as parts of geometric dimensioning and tolerancing. State the functional relationship and the applicable drawing system so manufacturing and inspection can interpret it consistently.

Supplemental source: [ASME Y14.5 overview](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-5-dimensioning-tolerancing). This summary does not implement its clauses or establish drawing compliance.

## Standards editions and service conditions
<!-- keys: standard edition, ISO GPS, general tolerance, thermal fit, legacy drawing -->
Catalog policy: retain the full standard designation and edition stated by the drawing. The [general linear tolerance guide](iso-general-linear-tolerances.md) records the legacy basis of existing table keys and the separate newer publication. Do not silently reinterpret those keys under another edition or mix ISO and ASME defaults.

For polymer mating parts, [Mitsubishi Chemical's dimensional-stability guidance](https://www.mcam.com/en/support/material-properties/dimensional-stability) explains how temperature and moisture can affect dimensions. See [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md). A modeled clearance result does not establish a manufactured fit throughout service.
