## Family identity and the cited product
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: virgin UHMW, unfilled polyethylene, TIVAR 1000 -->
UHMW-PE is ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene. This source describes white TIVAR 1000 Virgin stock, not every recycled, filled or modified UHMW grade. Keep that product identity when using its data. The sheet's issue date is 2026-09-28.

Compare [HDPE stock](polyethylene-hdpe.md) as a separate material family entry.

## Selection for sliding and wear components
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: wear strip, chain guide, bushing, scraper, low friction, abrasion -->
Mitsubishi Chemical lists bearings, bushings, chain guides, rollers and scrapers among uses for this product. Its wear, impact and friction characteristics motivate those choices. Friction and wear results still belong to their test conditions, not every mating surface or operating speed.

## Product density and comparison limits
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: density, specific gravity, ISO 1183-1, indicative values -->
For TIVAR 1000 Virgin, the source row reads: "Density ISO 1183-1 g/cm³ 0.93". This is a product-specific indicative density of 0.93 g/cm³. The sheet describes its data as comparison information for dry material, not guaranteed specification limits or a sufficient design basis on their own.
