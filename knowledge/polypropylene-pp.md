## Family identity and standard stock
<!-- keys: polypropylene, unreinforced, TECAFINE PP natural, rod, plate -->
Ensinger describes PP as a semi-crystalline thermoplastic and lists unreinforced TECAFINE PP natural and grey separately from TECAPRO MT. Identify the actual product and form before selecting numerical data; the family name does not specify additives or processing history.

## Selection for chemical and lightweight components
<!-- keys: chemical resistance, lightweight, low water absorption, welding, fittings -->
Ensinger highlights low moisture uptake, chemical resistance, electrical insulation and weldability, with chemical equipment and fittings among its applications. These traits make PP worth considering where the exposure and mechanical demands match the chosen grade.

Catalog application: check the actual fluid and service conditions rather than treating chemical resistance as compatibility with every medium.

## Temperature and dimensional limitations
<!-- keys: thermal expansion, cold impact, low temperature toughness, close tolerance -->
Ensinger lists substantial thermal expansion and reduced toughness in subzero conditions as considerations for PP. Low water uptake alone therefore does not establish a close fit or impact performance throughout service.

The manufacturer describes special heat stabilization for TECAPRO MT. Do not transfer that medical product's processing claims to standard TECAFINE PP stock.

See [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md).
