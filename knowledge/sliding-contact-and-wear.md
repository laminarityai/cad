## Mating surfaces and friction evidence
<!-- keys: counterface, surface roughness, coefficient of friction, static friction, dynamic friction -->
Mitsubishi Chemical Group describes wear as material loss under repeated mechanical contact. Contact type, mating materials, roughness and clearance influence behavior. Static and dynamic friction describe different stages of movement.

Catalog application: identify the counterface and test conditions before comparing coefficients; a low friction value alone does not establish service life.

## Pressure speed and frictional heating
<!-- keys: bearing pressure, sliding speed, pressure velocity, PV limit -->
Mitsubishi Chemical Group identifies applied pressure and movement velocity as wear variables, along with heat generated by friction.

Catalog policy: obtain supplier bearing data for the intended pressure, speed, duty and temperature. This guide supplies no universal pressure-velocity limit, allowable load or wear-life calculation. Keep missing limits unknown rather than borrowing them from a different grade or test.

## Lubrication moisture and chemicals
<!-- keys: dry sliding, lubricated sliding, wet bearing, wear environment -->
Mitsubishi Chemical Group includes lubricant type, moisture, chemicals and sunlight among environmental influences on wear behavior.

Catalog application: record dry or lubricated operation and the actual exposure when evaluating a material pair. See [corrosion and environmental compatibility](corrosion-and-environment.md), [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md) and [elastomer selection](elastomer-selection.md) for related compatibility questions.

## Candidate polymers and limits of comparison
<!-- keys: acetal bearings, nylon bearings, UHMW wear strip, POM PA -->
Mitsubishi Chemical Group identifies acetal, nylon and UHMW polyethylene among wear-resistant engineering plastics. This family-level guidance does not establish interchangeability between grades.

Catalog navigation: compare [POM-H](acetal-pom-h.md), [POM-C](mcam-ertacetal-c.md), [PA6 and PA66](nylon-pa6-pa66.md) and [UHMW-PE](polyethylene-uhmw.md). Consult [unfilled PTFE](ptfe-unfilled.md) separately rather than inferring its bearing performance from friction alone.
