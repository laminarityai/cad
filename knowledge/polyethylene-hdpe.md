## HDPE identity and stock forms

Ensinger identifies TECAFINE PE 300 as PE-HD and offers polyethylene stock shapes for machining. Its family list separately identifies high-molecular-weight and ultra-high-molecular-weight grades. Keep the selected stock designation rather than treating polyethylene as one interchangeable material.

## Selection considerations
<!-- keys: low moisture absorption, chemical resistance, electrical insulation, impact -->
Ensinger describes polyethylene's low moisture uptake, chemical resistance, electrical insulation and impact behavior. These are useful selection considerations for HDPE, but the family overview is not a guarantee for every fluid, loading condition or grade.

Catalog application: compare candidate stock against the actual environment and required stiffness before choosing it for a part.

## Machining and service dimensions
<!-- keys: thermal expansion, creep, close tolerance, machining -->
Low moisture uptake addresses one dimensional influence; it does not establish stability under heat or sustained loading. Assess temperature and duration for close-fitting polymer parts, and distinguish the modeled clearance from the service fit.

Supplemental source: [Mitsubishi Chemical dimensional-stability guidance](https://www.mcam.com/en/support/material-properties/dimensional-stability). See [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md) for sustained-load evidence and [CNC guidance](cnc-design-guidance.md) for machining context.
