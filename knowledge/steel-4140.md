## Identity includes product form and condition
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: alloy steel, bar, plate, cold finished, hot rolled -->
Alro's stock matrix lists 4140 in several forms and conditions, including cold-finished annealed, cold-finished quenched-and-tempered, hot-rolled annealed and hot-rolled quenched-and-tempered. The grade number alone does not select a finished material state.

This is a guide to distinguishing conditions, not a stock-availability promise or numerical specification.

## Annealed stock and subsequent treatment
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: annealed 4140, machining before heat treatment, final condition -->
The cited Alro matrix identifies annealed 4140 separately from quenched-and-tempered material. Catalog application: when machining annealed stock before further treatment, identify the required final condition and obtain evidence for that state; the incoming-stock description cannot establish finished-part strength.

See [material identity](material-identity.md).

## Quenched tempered and prehard descriptions
<!-- keys: quench and temper, Q and T, strength toughness, hardness -->
Ovako explains that quenching followed by tempering develops a balance of strength and toughness, with the result depending on treatment. This is process guidance, not a heat-treatment recipe for an unspecified 4140 part.

Supplemental source: [Ovako quenching and tempering steel](https://www.ovako.com/en/steel-portfolio/steel-grades/quenching-and-tempering-steel/).

Catalog application: require the supplier's stated condition and property or hardness specification when evaluating stock described as prehard. That label alone does not select a yield strength.

## Evidence needed for a numerical input
<!-- keys: yield strength, tensile strength, hardness conversion, grade equivalence -->
Alro's cited matrix establishes condition distinctions, not mechanical design values. Catalog policy: obtain the exact product specification, section size and treatment condition before proposing strength data. Do not infer strength from a trade description or assume equivalence with another designation.

Missing values stay unknown until supported research is verified. See [1018 steel](steel-1018.md) for a different steel choice and [property interpretation](material-properties.md) for comparison limits.
