## Stock thickness material and bend radius
<!-- keys: sheet thickness, uniform thickness, bend radius, temper, grain direction -->
Start with a consistent sheet thickness and identify the alloy and temper. Feasible bend geometry depends on the stock and forming process. Protolabs lists different materials and tooling requirements; a radius suitable for one combination is not a universal value for all sheet.

Source: Protolabs Sheet Metal Fabrication Design Guidelines. See [material identity](material-identity.md).

## Holes slots and features near bends
<!-- keys: hole to bend distance, slot distortion, flange, bend line -->
Features near a bend can distort during forming. Place holes and slots with the material, thickness, radius and tooling in mind. Where placement is functionally fixed, discuss a suitable operation sequence with the manufacturer.

Source: Protolabs Sheet Metal Fabrication Design Guidelines. A clearance-hole diameter alone does not establish a safe distance from a bend.

## Bend relief and forming access
<!-- keys: bend relief, corner relief, tearing, flange length, tool clearance -->
Reliefs at interrupted bends help accommodate forming. Flange dimensions and surrounding geometry must permit the chosen tools to engage and complete the sequence. Protolabs' tooling table gives conditional requirements for its service; retain those conditions if using a particular recommendation.

Supplemental source: [Protolabs Sheet Metal Fabrication Toolkit](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/design-for-sheet-metal-fabrication-toolkit/).

## Formed dimensions and drawing intent
<!-- keys: formed dimension, flat pattern, bend allowance, K factor, inspection -->
Distinguish the dimensions of the finished bent part from the flat pattern used to manufacture it. SOLIDWORKS describes bend allowance and bend deduction as ways of deriving flat stock length for the desired bent dimensions. Select parameters appropriate to the forming process.

Supplemental source: [SOLIDWORKS bend allowance and bend deduction](https://help.solidworks.com/2024/english/SolidWorks/sldworks/c_Bend_Allowance_and_Bend_Deduction.htm).
