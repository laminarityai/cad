## Density
<!-- page: 2 -->
This is the data sheet for Polymaker PolyTerra PLA+, the toughened grade. It is a different
product from plain PolyTerra, and its density differs. The physical properties table lists
"Density ISO1183, GB/T1033 1.24 g/cm3 at 21.5°C".

## How it differs from the plain grade
<!-- page: 2 -->
The sheet says "PolyTerra™ PLA+ filament contains organic material than ordinary PLA which brings
better layer adhesion and extra toughness." The source text reads this way, without a comparative
before "organic material".
