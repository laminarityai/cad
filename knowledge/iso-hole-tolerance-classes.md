## Hole basis and tolerance grade
<!-- keys: hole basis, nominal size, shaft fit, tolerance zone -->
The cited secondary reference explains IT grades and the distinction between the width of a tolerance zone and its position. The product table implements H hole classes with the lower limit at nominal size and the upper limit above nominal by the selected grade width. A hole class alone does not specify the mating shaft or establish an interference or clearance fit.

## H7 hole tolerance
<!-- keys: hole H7, H7 tolerance, bore tolerance, precision -->
Exact tolerance-table key: `H7`. Read the nominal diameter range as well as the class. The table stores the grade width in micrometres and applies it above nominal; it is not a symmetric plus/minus allowance.

For the same nominal-size interval, increasing the grade number widens the tolerance. Select the stated class rather than silently replacing it with another grade.

## Size intervals and supported coverage
<!-- keys: size range, boundary, units, micrometres, ISO 286, ASME, GD&T -->
Product-table snapshot: the supported H classes cover positive nominal diameters through 120 mm. Each interval excludes its lower endpoint and includes its upper endpoint. At a shared boundary, use the interval ending at that diameter. The source lists additional sizes and grades that are not thereby supported in the product.

Read limits from the exact table range; do not extrapolate, round to a neighboring range, or read a micrometre entry as millimetres. These classes are not an implementation of inch fits or geometric tolerancing.
