## Identity and Condition A
<!-- page: 1-7 -->
<!-- keys: solution treated 17-4, precipitation hardening, age hardening -->
Carpenter identifies Custom 630 as martensitic precipitation-hardening stainless, associated with UNS S17400. Condition A is solution treated; subsequent aging selects a different material state. Carpenter advises against service in Condition A without aging because of stress-corrosion cracking susceptibility.

Catalog application: keep grade, product form and final treatment together. Condition A does not establish properties for an aged part. See [material identity](material-identity.md).

## H900 strength and toughness comparison
<!-- page: 6 -->
<!-- keys: H900, high hardness 17-4, aging condition -->
Carpenter's typical mechanical-property table distinguishes H900 from other aging conditions. It reports higher hardness and lower impact energy for H900 than H1150 in the listed comparison.

Catalog policy: this comparison is not a guaranteed design allowable. Require the applicable product specification, test basis and final condition before choosing numerical strength or toughness data.

## H1150 and H1150M are distinct conditions
<!-- page: 6-7 -->
<!-- keys: H1150, H1150M, toughness, overaged 17-4 -->
Carpenter lists H1150 and H1150M separately, including different treatment schedules and typical properties. They are not interchangeable condition labels.

Catalog application: retain the full condition suffix in material requests and evidence. Do not combine a strength value from one aging condition with toughness from another. See [property interpretation](material-properties.md).
