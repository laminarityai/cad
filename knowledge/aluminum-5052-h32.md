## Identity and H32 temper
<!-- page: 2 -->
<!-- keys: alloy, temper, rolled sheet, coil, non heat treatable -->
This guide concerns rolled 5052 aluminum sheet and coil in H32 temper. BlueScope distinguishes H32, H38 and annealed O stock; their mechanical and forming data are separate. The alloy is described as non-heat-treatable, so do not interpret its temper as the same strengthening route as the existing [6061-T6 entry](kaiser-6061-t6.md).

## Selection for formed sheet parts
<!-- page: 2 -->
<!-- keys: bent bracket, enclosure, fabrication, weldability, corrosion -->
BlueScope describes 5052 as suitable for fabrication, with useful corrosion resistance and weldability. It is a candidate for formed sheet components when those characteristics matter. Application examples are selection context, not qualification of a particular welded or loaded part.

Use the [sheet-metal guide](sheet-metal-design-guidance.md) to connect material selection to geometry.

## Bending limits depend on the operation
<!-- page: 3 -->
<!-- keys: minimum bend radius, air bending, sheet thickness, cold forming -->
BlueScope's bend recommendations distinguish thickness and temper and apply to cold forming with press-brake air-bend dies. Other operations and tooling can change the outcome. Preserve that context rather than assigning a single bend radius to all 5052-H32 stock.

A proposed 5251 substitution also needs review; BlueScope treats it as a different alloy.
