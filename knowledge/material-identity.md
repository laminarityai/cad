## Grade and temper belong together
<!-- keys: alloy, temper, heat treatment, material designation -->
Kaiser's sheet, coil and plate datasheet separates the mechanical properties of its alloy by temper. Preserve the grade, temper and stock form when identifying a material; a family name alone does not select a property row. This is guidance on reading the manufacturer source, not a material specification.

See the existing [Kaiser material entry](kaiser-6061-t6.md) for the exact materials-table identity and its cited fields.

## Heat treatment and product form
<!-- keys: annealed, precipitation hardened, condition, H900, H1150 -->
Carpenter's precipitation-hardening stainless datasheet distinguishes supplied and aged conditions. Properties from one condition should not be assigned to another merely because the alloy designation matches. Record the condition of the finished part, including any subsequent treatment.

Supplemental source: [Carpenter Custom 630 datasheet](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/hubfs/7407324/Material%20Saftey%20Data%20Sheets/Custom%20630%20%2817-4%20PH%29.pdf). This section does not add a stainless material to the numerical table.

## Polymer families and manufacturer grades
<!-- keys: resin family, POM-H, POM-C, homopolymer, copolymer, Delrin -->
Ensinger distinguishes acetal homopolymer from copolymer and lists modified grades separately. Keep polymer type, manufacturer grade and reinforcement with the identity. A trade name or informal family alias is a search aid, not proof that two datasheets describe interchangeable stock.

Supplemental source: [Ensinger POM guidance](https://www.ensingerplastics.com/en/thermoplastic-materials/pom). Compare with the existing [acetal copolymer entry](mcam-ertacetal-c.md).

## Catalog use and unresolved identity
<!-- keys: exact key, missing grade, source provenance, material selection -->
Catalog policy: record the source revision, product designation, form and condition beside any proposed property. When the identity is unresolved, explain the selection uncertainty rather than borrowing a nearby material's values. Search provides reasons and pointers; checks obtain inputs through exact table keys or the server's researched-value workflow.
