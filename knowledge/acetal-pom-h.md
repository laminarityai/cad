## Homopolymer identity and the cited stock
<!-- keys: POM-H, Delrin, TECAFORM AD, unfilled acetal -->
Ensinger identifies TECAFORM AD natural as white acetal homopolymer made using Delrin resin. Keep the stock product and polymer type with its evidence. This page does not describe every Delrin grade or filled acetal formulation.

The existing [Ertacetal C entry](mcam-ertacetal-c.md) describes copolymer stock; its exact table key is a different identity.

## Selection and distinction from POM-C
<!-- keys: POM-H versus POM-C, dimensional stability, machining, sliding, wear -->
Ensinger describes TECAFORM AD natural as machinable, with useful sliding and wear properties. It attributes differences in density, hardness and strength relative to POM-C to crystallinity. Treat that comparison as selection context, not a fixed conversion between the two materials' properties.

See [CNC guidance](cnc-design-guidance.md).

## POM-H product density and evidence limits
<!-- keys: density, specific gravity, manufacturer product page -->
The TECAFORM AD natural product page lists "Density" and "1.43 g/cm3". The value is for that identified product. The web summary does not state a density test method or temperature alongside it; those details are unresolved here.

Catalog policy: this is a cited product observation, not a universal acetal value or a new numerical table row.
