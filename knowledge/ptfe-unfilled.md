## Virgin PTFE and modified grades
<!-- keys: polytetrafluoroethylene, virgin PTFE, unfilled, TECAFLON -->
Ensinger identifies TECAFLON PTFE natural as virgin PTFE, distinct from its glass-filled and polyimide-modified materials. Record the selected grade and stock process. A property of a modified bearing material does not automatically describe unfilled PTFE.

## Chemical and sliding applications
<!-- keys: low friction, chemical resistance, bushing, sliding, electrical insulation -->
Ensinger describes low friction, chemical resistance and electrical insulation as PTFE characteristics, with chemically exposed sliding components among its uses. These are selection reasons. Actual compatibility and wear behavior still depend on the product, mating surface and service environment.

## Structural and joining limitations
<!-- keys: low stiffness, thermal expansion, bonding, creep, cold flow -->
Ensinger highlights relatively low strength and rigidity, high thermal expansion and difficulty bonding PTFE. A low-friction material is not automatically a stiff structural replacement. Check deformation and retention under the intended loading rather than selecting on friction alone.

For sustained-load and fit considerations, see [polymer service conditions](polymer-service-conditions.md) and [fits and tolerances](fits-and-tolerances.md).

## Product evidence and unknown inputs
<!-- keys: density, tensile strength, service temperature, test conditions -->
The family page distinguishes virgin and filled products, but this guide establishes no universal PTFE mechanical dataset or continuous-service limit. Catalog policy: obtain the selected product's data, specimen condition and test method before researching a numerical input.

Missing supported values stay unknown; this page does not add a table row. Compare the separate [UHMW-PE guide](polyethylene-uhmw.md) by application and evidence, not by treating the families as equivalents.
