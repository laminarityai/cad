## Identity and machining use
<!-- page: 1 -->
<!-- keys: free machining stainless, sulfur, turned parts, screw machine -->
Carpenter identifies CarTech 303 as a sulfur-modified, free-machining stainless steel associated with UNS S30300. Its application examples include shafts, valve components and fittings. Modified Project 70+ is a separate product choice.

Catalog application: record the purchased product and condition; a machining benefit does not establish suitability for every service.

## Corrosion tradeoff against 304
<!-- keys: 303 versus 304, corrosion resistance, machinability tradeoff -->
Carpenter's supplemental [austenitic machining guidance](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/blog/ideas-for-machining-austenitic-stainless-steels) explains that sulfur improves machinability but reduces corrosion resistance and weldability compared with conventional austenitic grades.

Catalog application: compare [304](cliffs-304.md) and [316](cliffs-316.md) against the actual exposure rather than substituting 303 on machinability alone. See [corrosion and environmental compatibility](corrosion-and-environment.md).

## Welding and fabrication limits
<!-- page: 4 -->
<!-- keys: 303 welding, hot cracking, welded assembly -->
Carpenter does not recommend welding 303 because sulfur promotes hot cracking.

Catalog application: flag a proposed welded assembly for material and process review. This guide does not supply a welding procedure or treat a supplier's discussion of possible fabrication methods as production qualification.

## Condition and property evidence
<!-- page: 2 -->
<!-- keys: cold worked 303, annealed 303, strength, heat treatment -->
Carpenter states that 303 does not harden through heat treatment but gains strength and hardness through cold work.

Catalog policy: identify the incoming form and condition before accepting mechanical data. This guide provides no default yield strength or allowable stress. Use the exact product evidence and [property interpretation](material-properties.md); missing values remain unknown.
