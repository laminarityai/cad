## Preload torque and friction
<!-- keys: bolt preload, tightening torque, lubrication, friction, clamping force -->
Tightening torque is an indirect means of producing preload. Thread and bearing-surface friction, lubrication and coatings affect the relationship. A torque value selected only from nominal screw diameter lacks essential context.

Source: NASA Fastener Design Manual, Torque Formulas. This foundational manual is historical; use current product-specific assembly instructions for a selected fastener.

## Joint stiffness fatigue and thread engagement
<!-- keys: joint stiffness, fatigue loading, thread engagement, stripping, pullout -->
Bolt and clamped-part stiffness influence how an external tensile load changes bolt load while the joint remains clamped. Consider separation, fatigue, combined loading and thread pullout where relevant. Engagement depends on thread geometry and the strength of both mating materials; a universal multiple of diameter is not a complete joint design.

Source: NASA Fastener Design Manual, Design Criteria. The [clearance-hole](metric-clearance-holes.md) and [tap-drill](metric-tapped-holes.md) entries establish neither joint capacity nor preload.

## Insert hole and boss geometry
<!-- keys: insert pilot hole, boss diameter, pullout resistance, torque resistance -->
SPIROL distinguishes insert designs and installation methods. Select the exact insert before specifying its receiving hole and boss. External geometry, polymer and surrounding material affect pullout and torque resistance. The screw's thread size alone does not define the insert's installation hole.

Supplemental source: [SPIROL Inserts for Plastics guide](https://www.spirol.com/assets/files/SPIROL-Inserts-for-Plastics-us.pdf), Insert Design and Design Guidelines.

## Heat installation and plastic relaxation
<!-- keys: heat set, ultrasonic insertion, thermoplastic, thermoset, stress relaxation -->
Heat and ultrasonic insertion rely on thermoplastic flow around the insert; that mechanism does not apply to thermosets. Installation alignment and the recommended hole geometry matter. Plastic relaxation also needs consideration in maintaining a clamped joint; adding a metal thread does not establish the performance of the entire assembly.

Supplemental source: [SPIROL Inserts for Plastics guide](https://www.spirol.com/assets/files/SPIROL-Inserts-for-Plastics-us.pdf), Plastics Overview and Design Guidelines.
