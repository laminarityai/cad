## Density
<!-- page: 1 -->
This is the data sheet for Polymaker PolyTerra PLA, the plain grade. It is a different product
from the toughened plus grade, which has its own data sheet and its own density. The sheet lists
the row
"Density" with the value "1.31g/cm3 at 23°C".
