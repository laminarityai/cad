## Wall thickness and transitions
<!-- keys: uniform walls, sink marks, voids, warpage, coring -->
Protolabs recommends consistent walls and coring unnecessary mass. Uneven cooling around thick sections can contribute to sink, voids and warping; gradual transitions help reduce these problems.

Catalog application: choose wall dimensions with the molder for the selected resin and flow path. This guide does not establish a universal minimum wall or thickness ratio.

## Draft and mold release
<!-- keys: draft angle, ejection, texture, undercuts, parting line -->
Protolabs explains that draft supports release, with surface texture affecting the requirement. Undercuts can require additional tooling mechanisms or geometry changes; ejector pins may leave marks.

Catalog application: identify the pull direction, parting line, finish and functional surfaces before agreeing release geometry. No single draft angle applies to every resin, depth or tool.

## Ribs bosses and assembly features
<!-- keys: rib thickness, screw bosses, gussets, standoffs, inserts -->
Protolabs recommends thin ribs or gussets for support and coring thick bosses to limit local mass. Bosses may locate screws, inserts or pins; thick junctions can cause sink or voids.

Catalog application: review the fastener interface and surrounding wall together with the molder. See [fastened joints and inserts](fastened-joints-and-inserts.md); a catalog fastener dimension does not establish a moldable boss.

## Shrinkage resin and dimensional tolerances
<!-- keys: mold shrinkage, molding shrinkage, resin grade, molded tolerances -->
Protolabs identifies resin shrinkage, cooling, part geometry and tool design as contributors to finished dimensions. Additives can change molding behavior and properties.

Catalog application: specify the actual molding grade and critical dimensions. Do not transfer filament or stock-shape data to a molded part without supporting evidence. See [material identity](material-identity.md), [fits and tolerances](fits-and-tolerances.md) and [polypropylene](polypropylene-pp.md).

## Gates and tooling evidence
<!-- keys: gate location, weld lines, mold flow, tooling review -->
Protolabs explains that gate location affects filling, weld lines and visible marks.

Catalog policy: obtain manufacturing review of the selected resin, geometry and functional surfaces before treating process guidance as a production requirement. These notes support research and supplier discussion; they do not add a moldability check, tooling specification or guaranteed tolerance to CAD.
