Agent reply: The density of **Polymaker PolyTerra PLA+ is 1.24 g/cm³ at 21.5 °C**, measured to ISO 1183 / GB/T 1033. **Source:** *PolyTerra PLA+ technical data sheet V5.1* (Polymaker), page 2, physical properties table, "Density ISO1183, GB/T1033 1.24 g/cm3 at 21.5°C". https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0789/2979/files/PolyTerra_PLA_Plus_EN_V5.1.pdf (retrieved 2026-11-07) **Don't mix it up with plain PolyTerra PLA.** The plain grade has its own data sheet (*PolyTerra PLA TDS V6.0*, page 1), which gives **1.31 g/cm³ at 23 °C**. That's about 6% higher. Check which spool you have. **Mass checks on your bracket:** PolyTerra PLA+ isn't in the materials table that the scoring tool uses, so it can't calculate the bracket's mass in this material. The closest entries are other PLAs, such as `prusament-pla` and `bambu-pla-basic`, but their densities are different. I can also work out the mass by hand from 1.24 g/cm³ once a scorecard gives the volume, but that number would be my own arithmetic, not a check. The density comes from Polymaker's data sheet, and nothing in this toolset has checked it. It's a candidate value until you confirm it. A printed part also usually weighs less than its solid volume × density, because of infill and perimeter settings.
